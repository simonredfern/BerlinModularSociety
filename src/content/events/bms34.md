**Many artists are building their own modules and this event gives you an opportunity to fully join in or hang out and get an insight into the scene followed by cool live modular performances.**

**Where: Klunkerkranich**

**When: Sunday 14 May 2023 from 16:00**

What can you expect? This is collaboration event with the 4th edition of the DIY Kit Day held at the rather special roof top club **Klunkerkranich.** There will be a DIY Kit Market, modular DIY workshops and of course live modular synthesizer performances. I.e. you can solder your own modules, hangout and / or / then party !

Address: Floor 5, Neukölln Arcaden, Karl-Marx-Straße 66, 12043 Berlin **→**  [map](https://goo.gl/maps/hN7NMaejueesBzdv6)

More info about the venue: [www.klunkerkranich.org](http://www.klunkerkranich.org/?fbclid=IwAR1iYw4ol-9DVKLGVuCePd-DvftLWP7Fs4_cREQUBeeLg-u2m39nVhj_bAE)

Note: This venue is not easy to find! You have to go into the Neukölln Arcaden shopping centre and take the lift up - then walk through the car park. There are super roof top areas and the club is inside. Look for the Wohnzimmer and the Club "Hinter den Alpen".

Please bring soldering irons, if you have some!There will be a supply of tools that can be borrowed, as well.
