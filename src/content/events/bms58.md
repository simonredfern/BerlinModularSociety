#### (Post Superbooth)

**DIY Kit Day** '26 took place in **Klunkerkranich**'s new beer garden, which is on the right after you come up the ramp. The DIY Kit market and soldering area will be open from 16:00 to 22:00h. Basic tools will be supplied, but please bring a **soldering iron**, if you have one. If you reserve a module in advance, that will get you a free [guest list](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1LFRGDQAN9vZROPxPbwyyqdBymEu3GTlyaWlfssImwFk/edit?usp=drivesdk) spot to avoid waiting in the line. Please be early. Manufacturers in the house will include: ATOV, Befaco, Clacktronics, Error Instruments, Exploding Shed, Krach der Roboter, Modulove, Noisio, Shakmat, SynthLab, Timo Rozendal, Zlob and a few surprize guests.

**The list of module kits available for reservation was** [HERE](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1LFRGDQAN9vZROPxPbwyyqdBymEu3GTlyaWlfssImwFk/edit?usp=drivesdk)
